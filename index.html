Design

We design and re-plan your home, whether it is one room or the whole house. We help you find the style that is right for you, build it in 3D and go through every plan and material with you.

Request consultation
01

Discovery call

A first conversation about your home: which spaces you want to change, what is not working today, your budget and your timeline.

02

Style direction

We help you decide what you want. Together we look at references, colors and finishes until the look of your home is clear.

03

3D design

We re-plan the space and build it in 3D, so you can see your home from every angle before anything is ordered or built.

04

Plan review

We sit down with you and go through the full plan and the 3D views, room by room, and adjust until everything is right.

05

Materials

We go over every material you want, from cabinets and stone to tile, flooring, fixtures and lighting. You leave with a complete design, ready to build.

Construction · optional

Installation and construction are a separate service from design, and they are optional. If you want us to build what we designed, we bring our contractors and manage the work. If you have your own contractor, that works too.

Request consultation
01

Your choice

When the design is complete, you decide who builds it: our contractors or your own. The design is yours either way.

02

Estimate & schedule

If you build with us, you get a clear estimate and a timeline before any work starts.

03

Orders

We order the materials from your design, receive them and check every delivery before installation.

04

Construction & installation

Our contractors do the work, and we follow it closely so the result matches the design.

05

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished space with you and take care of any last details.
