Design Portfolio

Design

From the first idea to the final finish, we design your home with care and precision. We lead each step for you, so the process feels calm and the result feels like yours.

Request consultation
01

Discovery call

A first conversation about your home: which spaces you want to change, what is not working today, your budget and your timeline.

02

Style direction

We help you decide what you want. Together we look at references, colors and finishes until the look of your home is clear.

03

3D design & review

We re-plan the space and build it in 3D, so you can see your home from every angle. Then we walk through the plan with you, room by room, and refine it until everything is right.

04

Final selections

We go over every material with you, from cabinets and stone to tile, flooring, fixtures and lighting. Once you approve the plan and the selections, your design is complete and ready to build.

05

Construction support

We stay close while your home is built, keeping the thinking behind every drawing and coordinating with your builder, until the day the space stands complete.

01

Bare room

Every project starts here: an empty space, measured and planned down to the last inch.

02

Cabinetry

Cabinet boxes and the island go in, exactly where the 3D plan placed them.

03

Stone & finishes

Doors, countertops and backsplash follow, in the materials you approved.

04

Finished

Fixtures, lighting and furniture complete the room you saw in 3D before it was built.
