Process

From the first idea to the final finish, we design your space with care and precision. We lead each step for you, so the process feels calm and the result feels like yours.

01

Discovery call

A first conversation about your project: what you want to change, what is not working today, your budget and your timeline.

02

Style direction

We help you decide what you want. Together we look at references, colors and finishes until the look of your space is clear.

03

3D design & review

We re-plan the space and build it in 3D, so you can see it from every angle. Then we walk through the plan with you, area by area, and refine it until everything is right.

04

Final selections

We go over every material with you, from cabinets and stone to tile, flooring, fixtures and lighting. Before we close the design, we check every detail once more. Once you approve the plan and the selections, it is complete and ready to build.

05

Construction support

We work with a team of contractors we trust, and we can bring them in to carry out the work. We stay with you the whole way, keeping every detail true to the design until the space stands complete.
